# Rule: Content Writing & Humanizer Standards (MANDATORY)

Whenever writing, drafting, generating, or editing **any content** (including blog posts, service page copy, website text, articles, guides, or social snippets), the agent MUST strictly follow these rules:

---

## 1. 100% Adherence to the Humanizer Skill
You must strictly adhere to the guidelines set in:
👉 [Humanizer Skill](file:///c:/xampp/htdocs/seoservice/.agents/skills/humanizer/SKILL.md)

Every generated or edited sentence must pass the Humanizer test:
- **No Staging**: Never use *"Not X, but Y"*, *"Not only X, but also Y"*, or clipped tails (*"..., no guessing"*). State points directly.
- **No Dramatic Closers & Fragments**: Eliminate repetitive one-line summary paragraphs (*"That is the real win."*, *"Let that sink in."*).
- **No Shallow Aphorisms**: Ban *"at its core"*, *"the heart of the matter"*, *"X is the Y of Z"*, etc.
- **No Staged Run-ups**: Eliminate *"Let's dive in"*, *"Here's what you need to know"*, *"Honestly?"*, *"Look"*.
- **No Forced Triads**: Stop grouping items into artificial threes.
- **No Dashes (— or –)**: Do NOT use em dashes or en dashes as lazy clause connectors. Use periods, commas, colons, or clean sentence splits.
- **Strictly Banned AI Words**: Never use:
  - `delve`, `deep dive`, `landscape` (abstract), `testament`, `tapestry`, `vibrant`, `crucial`, `robust`, `pivotal`, `intricate`, `meticulous`, `highlight`, `underscore`, `enhance`, `bolster`, `showcase`, `interplay`, `quietly`.
- **No AI Send-offs / Padding**: Avoid *"The future looks bright"*, *"Challenges and Legacy"*, or generic cheerleading.
- **No Formatting Decoration**: No unnecessary bold words in paragraphs, no emojis in professional headings, no bold labels on every list bullet.

---

## 2. The 1% Natural Human Imperfection Rule
To ensure the writing reads 100% authentically human and never triggers AI-pattern detectors:
- Maintain **~1% natural human grammar quirk / conversational cadence**.
- Allow natural colloquial phrasing, varied sentence lengths (mixing short punchy statements with longer flowing thoughts), organic fragments, or mild conversational rhythm shifts that real human writers naturally make.
- Never write mechanically rigid or algorithmically sterile prose.

---

## 3. Truthful & Factual Integrity
- Keep all real facts, names, steps, and data accurate.
- Never hallucinate fake stats, made-up quotes, or phantom case studies.
- When covering Abdullah's SEO services, maintain the authentic, expert practitioner voice.

---

## 4. Visual Media Standard (3 In-Body Images + Owner Thumbnail)
- **Thumbnail**: Designed and provided by the owner. Used as the main featured thumbnail for the post header and cards; never duplicated inside the body text.
- **In-Content Images**: Every blog post body MUST contain exactly **3 distinct, professionally designed related images** (workflow diagrams, architecture infographics, or process maps) spaced evenly across the sections.
- **Border Radius**: Strictly **4px** (`border-radius: 4px; border: 1px solid #e2e8f0;`) across all visual containers, cards, and figures.
